About the Project

Luxury homes
by the river

Shelter Riverside is a landmark residential-cum-commercial project in Sector 14, Taloja — ideally positioned on a 20-metre wide corner plot alongside the Taloja River. The project features twin G+15 towers with a unique advantage: 50% of all flats directly face the riverside, offering serene, unobstructed water views.

With an Air Conditioned Entrance Lobby, AC in every bedroom, high-speed branded elevators, and a generous 2nd-floor landscaped podium garden, Riverside sets a new benchmark for affordable luxury in Navi Mumbai.
